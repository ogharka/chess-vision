Analysis runs on your device with Stockfish 18 (GPLv3); nothing is sent to a server. "Full strength" downloads the complete 113 MB engine once and keeps it in your browser; choose "Lite" on mobile data. A forced win, loss or draw is only reported when the search proves it (a mate score, or a line that ends in stalemate, repetition or dead material). Everything else is the engine's estimate at the depth shown, never a guarantee.
